About Rooted Love

Established in 2026, Rooted Love is a small organization of very dedicated botanists and photographers. We aim to paint a picture of the world around us through the flora that sprouts all around the world, starting with southeastern America.

Future Goals:

  • Merch Store
  • Physical Location for Flower Displays
  • More Additions and Branches to Team

If you wish to submit flower photos of your own, please contact us at submissions@rootedlove.com!
